# AfyaPlus Health - Security Audit Schedule

## Overview
This document sets out when and how AfyaPlus administrators carry out security audits of clinic systems.

## Audit schedule
- Administrators run a full security audit every quarter, that is once every three months.
- A lightweight access-review audit is run on the first Monday of every month.
- An emergency audit is triggered within 48 hours of any reported data breach.

## What an audit covers
- Review of user access rights and removal of accounts for staff who have left.
- Check that encryption is enabled on all servers and portable devices.
- Review of system logs for unusual or unauthorised access.
- Verification that data backups completed successfully.

## Responsibilities
The IT Administrator leads each audit. Findings are recorded in the audit register and shared with the Data Protection Officer.

## Follow-up
Any high-risk finding must be fixed within 7 days. Medium-risk findings must be fixed within 30 days. All fixes are verified in the next scheduled audit.
