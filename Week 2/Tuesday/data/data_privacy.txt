AfyaPlus Health - Data Privacy Rules

1. Purpose
These rules govern how AfyaPlus collects, stores, and uses patient and staff data across all clinics in Kenya.

2. Core data privacy rules
Patient records may only be accessed by staff directly involved in that patient's care.
All personal data must be encrypted both in storage and in transit.
Patient data must never be shared with third parties without the patient's written consent.
Staff must use unique login credentials and must not share passwords.
Any data stored on portable devices must be encrypted and password protected.

3. Consent
Patients must give informed consent before their data is collected. Consent can be withdrawn at any time, after which the data is archived and no longer used for analysis.

4. Data retention
Patient medical records are retained for 7 years after the last visit, then securely deleted. Staff HR records are retained for 7 years after employment ends.

5. Breach reporting
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours of discovery.

6. Legal basis
AfyaPlus complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and the patient has the right to access, correct, or request deletion of their personal data.
