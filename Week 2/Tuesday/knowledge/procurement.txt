AfyaPlus Health - Procurement and Purchase Approval Policy

1. Purpose
This policy sets out how AfyaPlus staff request, approve, and record purchases of goods and services for clinic operations.

2. Approval thresholds
Procurement under 500 US dollars can be approved by the team lead alone.
Clinic equipment purchases above 500 US dollars require director sign-off.
Logistics hardware purchases between 1,000 and 2,000 US dollars require the Procurement Officer's signature.
Any purchase above 2,000 US dollars requires both the Procurement Officer and the Finance Director to sign off.

3. Supplier selection
At least three quotations must be obtained for any purchase above 1,000 US dollars. Preference is given to approved suppliers on the AfyaPlus vendor register.

4. Cold-chain items
Vaccines, test kits, and other cold-chain items must be routed to the Refrigerated Depot (Section X) and handled by the assigned inventory representative.

5. General inventory
Non-cold-chain supplies are routed to the General Inventory Depot (Section Y).

6. Purchase orders
No goods may be ordered without an approved purchase order. Orders placed without a valid purchase order will not be paid.

7. Emergency purchases
In a clinical emergency, a purchase may proceed with verbal approval from a director, provided a written purchase order is raised within 24 hours.

8. Payment terms
Standard supplier payment terms are 30 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Early payment discounts should be used where available.

9. Record keeping
All procurement records, including quotations, approvals, and invoices, must be kept for at least 7 years for audit and tax reporting.
