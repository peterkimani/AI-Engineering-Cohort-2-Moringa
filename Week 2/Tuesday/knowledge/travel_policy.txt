AfyaPlus Health - Staff Travel and Reimbursement Policy

1. Scope
This policy applies to all AfyaPlus staff who travel for clinic operations, outreach, training, or supplier visits across Kenya.

2. Approval before travel
All work travel must be approved in advance by the staff member's line manager. Travel to another county also requires approval from the Regional Operations Lead.

3. Reimbursable expenses
AfyaPlus reimburses economy public transport, fuel for authorised clinic vehicles, accommodation up to 6,000 Kenyan shillings per night, and meals up to 2,000 Kenyan shillings per day. Receipts are required for every claimed expense.

4. Non-reimbursable expenses
Personal entertainment, fines, upgrades to business or first class, and travel for non-work purposes are not reimbursed.

5. Claim submission
Travel claims must be submitted through the staff portal within 14 days of returning from the trip. A claim submitted after 30 days will not be processed except with written approval from the Finance Director.

6. Reimbursement timeline
AfyaPlus reimburses staff travel within 30 days of an approved claim. Payment is made to the staff member's registered bank account.

7. Mileage
Staff using a personal vehicle with prior approval are reimbursed at 55 Kenyan shillings per kilometre, based on the shortest practical route.

8. Advances
A travel advance of up to 70 percent of estimated costs may be requested at least 5 working days before travel. Any unused advance must be returned within 7 days of the trip ending.
